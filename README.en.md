# Everyday Life Together

**Negotiation and Repair in Intimate Relationships**

By KK · Complete Chinese first draft v0.1 · 3 November 2026 · Content accepted by author

The manuscript and rights notice retain their original first-draft wording. The current content-review status is stated here and in the metadata.

Fourteen original, story-led chapters explore money, household work, parents, children, personal space, physical intimacy, lasting differences, apologies, and trust. The book focuses on making needs discussable, sharing invisible work, and turning repair into observable change. It distinguishes ordinary disagreements from abuse, coercion, and controlling behavior.
